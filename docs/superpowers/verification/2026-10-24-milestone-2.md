## Scope and privacy qualifications

- Pre-removal downloader/manual-refresh probes were instrumented. They establish anonymous
  endpoint compatibility, not the mandatory post-removal application-refresh result.
- Task 8 is explicitly deferred by the owner. UI-owned strings are being catalogued;
  non-UI advice/provenance producers may still supply Chinese text. This known polish is
  follow-up work, not silently described as translated or used to weaken the UI AST gate.
- Runtime hosts and purposes are documented in `AGENTS.md`; no Tencent runtime fetches,
  remote control, identity, log upload or self-update remain. CommunityDragon is a
  community-hosted static source, not a Riot-owned CDN.
- Source migration changes portrait bytes, not classifier mathematics. Five pinned
  Data Dragon/client-image pairs match correctly; the original bundled bank has 165
  correct, eight abstentions and no wrong matches among 173 references. Combined-bank
  self-match coverage is not independent accuracy. Old bundled/client references remain
  available for rollback if the live bench check fails.
- Raw captures and request-observation files stay local and are not committed. Any future
  committed player capture must be redacted first.
